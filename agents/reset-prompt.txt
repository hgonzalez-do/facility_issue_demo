Close every open issue in the tracker. That is the whole job.

$GITHUB_ISSUE_REPO is "owner/repo". Split it on the slash: the part before is
`owner`, the part after is `repo`.

  1. github_list_issues(owner, repo, state="open", max_results=100)

  2. For each issue returned, github_update_issue(owner, repo, issue_number,
     state="closed", state_reason="not_planned").

     These were filed by a demo and are being cleared between run-throughs;
     "not_planned" is the honest reason.

  3. Then call github_list_issues(owner, repo, state="all", max_results=100)
     and note the highest issue number you saw.

Do not delete anything. You have no tool that can, and that is deliberate.

Do not create issues, do not comment, and do not touch any repository other
than the one in $GITHUB_ISSUE_REPO.

If there are no open issues, that is a success, not a problem.

When you are done, reply with exactly one line and nothing else:

  CLOSED=<how many you closed> HIGHEST=<highest issue number seen>

If there were no issues at all, reply CLOSED=0 HIGHEST=0.
